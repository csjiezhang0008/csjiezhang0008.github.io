**Research interests**
Dr. Zhang leads a research lab in game theory, focusing on algorithmic decision-making, incentive design, and agent coordination in both competitive and cooperative settings. His work develops analytical tools to align individual incentives with system-level goals, with the aim to optimize system performance at equilibrium. His research involves worst-case guarantees and beyond worst-case analysis, designing mechanisms that remain effective under a broad range of conditions.

Beyond game theory, Dr. Zhang also conducts research on blockchain protocols and their applications, machine learning, and multi-agent systems. He has received independent research funding as principal investigator from The Leverhulme Trust and EPSRC. His broader interests extend to the digital economy and optimization.
